## 🌍 Featured Project

### Monkey Eating Mango
**https://MonkeyEatingMango.com**

An AI-powered travel planner that creates personalized day-by-day itineraries in minutes. Instead of spending hours comparing blogs and maps, travelers answer a few simple questions and receive a complete itinerary with attractions, routes, budgets, local food recommendations, and exportable travel plans.

**Highlights**

* 🗺️ AI-generated personalized itineraries
* 📍 Interactive maps and optimized daily routes
* 🍜 Local food recommendations
* 💰 Budget estimates
* 📄 Free PDF, Excel, and Google Sheets exports
* 🌎 100+ curated destination itineraries
